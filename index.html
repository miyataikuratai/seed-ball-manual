What is a Seed Ball?

A simple way to sow seeds

Seed balls are small balls made from seeds, clay and soil. They are used as a method of planting seeds.

Why do we use seed balls?

Seed balls make seeds easier to handle and provide an alternative way of sowing.

Materials

Chili

Seeds of plants.

Compost

Organic material that supports plant growth.

Clay soil

Helps hold the seed ball together.

Rice bran

An organic material used in the mixture.

Eggshell

A material that contains calcium.

Water

Used to adjust the moisture of the mixture.
